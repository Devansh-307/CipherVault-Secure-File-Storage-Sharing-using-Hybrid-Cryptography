HYBRID CRYPTOGRAPHY IN DISTRIBUTED ZERO-TRUST ARCHITECTURES
============================================================
Author: Devansh Rathore
Research Group: Advanced Cybersecurity Engineering
Standard: NIST SP 800-38D & FIPS 180-4

1. EXECUTIVE ABSTRACT
In modern decentralized infrastructure, data confidentiality and integrity cannot rely on transport-layer security alone. End-to-end data-at-rest encryption utilizing Hybrid Cryptography provides mathematical guarantees against unauthorized inspection even in fully compromised hosting environments.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC CONSTRAINTS
- Bulk Encryption: AES-256 in Galois/Counter Mode (AES-GCM)
- Key Encapsulation Mechanism (KEM): RSA-2048/4096 with Optimal Asymmetric Encryption Padding (OAEP)
- Integrity Fingerprinting: SHA-256 Cryptographic Hash Digest
- Non-Repudiation: RSA-PSS Digital Signatures with Maximum Salt Length

3. EMPIRICAL BENCHMARKS
Empirical analysis demonstrates hybrid systems achieve over 95% of native AES throughput while maintaining mathematical non-disclosure guarantees of asymmetric public-key infrastructure.
